# Subject Matter Mathematics

A portfolio of **Grade 8 mathematics lesson designs, lesson plans and worksheets**, written as day-wise teaching material that a teacher can pick up and use.

Author: **Unsi Rathod**, M.Sc. Mathematics, B.Ed, 6 years of teaching experience (Grades 7 to 12, CBSE).

## What each chapter contains

Every chapter folder has the same three documents:

| Document | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Lesson Design** | Day-by-day flow of the chapter: concepts, worked examples, classwork (CW) and homework (HW) |
| **Lesson Plan** | Session-by-session plan with learning outcomes, resources, teacher and student activity with timings, assessment, homework, skills and intelligences addressed |
| **Worksheet** | Practice and check-for-understanding questions for the chapter |

## Chapters

| Folder | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| [A Square and A Cube](./A%20Square%20and%20A%20Cube) | Perfect squares and cubes, square roots and cube roots (12 sessions) |
| [Proportional Reasoning](./Proportional%20Reasoning) | Multi-term ratios, scale on maps, dividing a whole in a ratio, pie charts, direct and inverse proportion (10 sessions) |
| [Tales by Dots and Lines](./Tales%20by%20Dots%20and%20Lines) | Data handling: mean as a balance point, median, dot plots and line graphs |

## How the lessons are built

- **Real-life hooks first.** Chapters open with a familiar situation (idli batter, a spice mix, a road trip) before introducing the formal idea.
- **Clear learning outcomes** for every session, with a matching quick assessment and homework task.
- **Teacher and student time split** into a short teacher-led launch and a longer student activity.
- **Cross-multiplication, product rules and formulas are explained, not just stated**, with worked examples students can follow.

## Start here
